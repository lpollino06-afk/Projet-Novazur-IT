Frequently asked questions

Do you really speak English?

Yes. You can call us, write to us and be helped on site entirely in English, from the first call to the end of the visit. We also speak French, which helps when dealing with local providers.

Do you work in Monaco?

Yes. Monaco is part of our main service area, together with Menton, Cap-d'Ail, Beaulieu, Villefranche, Nice, Antibes and Cannes. We regularly visit homes and offices from Monte-Carlo to Fontvieille.

Can you help while I'm abroad?

Yes. Most software issues can be solved remotely, wherever you are. For your Riviera home, we can check the internet box, Wi-Fi, TV and cameras before you arrive, and coordinate with your caretaker or concierge.

Can you deal with French internet providers for me?

We can help you understand your contract, report a fault to your internet provider and set up the equipment they send, so you don't have to struggle with French helplines.

Do you support businesses?

Yes: IT maintenance and helpdesk, cybersecurity, Microsoft 365, backups and VoIP phone systems for small and medium-sized companies, including Monaco-based businesses and international teams in Sophia Antipolis.

Is my data kept confidential?

Absolutely. We only access what is needed to fix the issue, always with your consent, and we never keep copies of your data.
